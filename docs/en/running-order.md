# Running order

[Українською](../running-order.md) · English

The running order is a list of passages, songs, texts, and images in the order you will
show them.
This page is for the operator who prepares a show beforehand: how to build a running order,
show it item by item, and save it as a program for next time. How to show a single verse
without a running order is in [Show a text](show-text.md).

## Add items

To add an item to the running order:

- A passage: choose the verses and click the **Add the passage to the running order** icon
  above the chapter numbers, next to the passage's reference. The passage remembers the
  translations chosen at that moment.
- A song: open the song in the **Songs** panel and click the **Add to the running order**
  icon next to its title. The song remembers the choice of **As in the file** or
  **Plain text**.
- Custom text: in the **Text to screen** panel, type the text and click
  **To the running order**.
- An image: in the **Images** panel, point at a thumbnail and click the
  **Add to the running order** icon. The item remembers **Fit** or **Fill**.

New items go to the end of the running order.

How to open a song or custom text is in [Songs and custom text](songs-and-text.md).

## Open the running order

To open the running order, click the **Running order** icon at the top. A panel with the
list opens. The icon at the start of a row shows the kind of item: a book — a passage, a
note — a song, the letter T — custom text, a photo — an image.

![The Running order panel with three items: John, Romans, and “Announcement”; at the top,
the Next button](../img/en/running-order.png)

## Change the order

In the **Running order** panel:

- To move an item, drag it by the dotted handle at the start of its row, or click **Up** or
  **Down** in its row.
- To remove an item, click the trash icon **Remove** in its row.
- To remove all items, click **Clear the running order** at the top of the panel. In place
  of the list, “Running order cleared” appears with a **Cancel** button that brings every
  item back. You can cancel until you add a new item, open a program, or reload the control
  window.

## Show item by item

To show an item, click its row. The item appears on screen at once and is highlighted in
the list. A song also opens in the **Songs** panel, with its first slide on screen.

To go to the next item, click **Next** at the top of the panel; to the previous one, the
**Previous item** icon next to it. If nothing has been shown yet, **Next** starts with the
first item.

Inside an item, step through as usual: the arrow keys and a clicker go to the next verse or
song slide, and **Next** in the panel goes to the next item.

## Save a program

A program is a saved running order that you can open next time.

To save the running order as a program:

1. At the top of the panel, click the **Programs** icon.
2. In the **Program name** field, type a name, for example `Meeting on October 4`.
3. Click **Save**. A program with the same name is replaced by the new one.

Saved programs appear below the field. To open a program, click its name: it replaces the
current running order. If the list had something in it, “Opened: …” appears above it with a
**Cancel** button that brings back the previous list. You can cancel until you change the
new list — add, remove, or move an item — open another program, or reload the control
window.

To delete a program, click the trash icon in its row. In its place, “Deleted: …” appears
with a **Cancel** button that brings the program back. You can cancel until you delete
another program, save a new one, or reload the control window.

The running order and the programs are kept in the app's folder, so they stay after a
restart.
